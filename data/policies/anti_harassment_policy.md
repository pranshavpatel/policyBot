# Anti-Harassment and Anti-Discrimination Policy

See also Code of Conduct §2 for the general respectful-workplace expectation.

## 1. Prohibited Conduct
- Harassment or discrimination based on race, color, religion, sex, national origin, age, disability, sexual orientation, gender identity, or any other legally protected status is strictly prohibited.
- This includes verbal, physical, and visual harassment, as well as harassment conducted over Slack, email, or other digital channels.

## 2. Reporting
- Reports can be made to your manager, HR, or the anonymous ethics hotline — you are never required to report to the person you're reporting about, even if that's your manager.
- All reports are investigated promptly, typically within **10 business days** of receipt.

## 3. Confidentiality
- Reports are kept as confidential as possible, shared only with those who need to know to investigate and resolve the matter.

## 4. No Retaliation
- Retaliation against anyone who reports harassment or discrimination in good faith, or who participates in an investigation, is strictly prohibited and will result in disciplinary action.

## 5. Outcomes
- Substantiated violations result in disciplinary action up to and including immediate termination.
- HR will follow up with the reporting employee on the general outcome, within the bounds of confidentiality for the accused.
