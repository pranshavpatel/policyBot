# Bereavement Leave Policy

Full detail on bereavement leave; see also Leave Policy §9 for a summary.

## 1. Immediate Family
- Employees receive **3 paid days** of bereavement leave for the death of an immediate family member (spouse, child, parent, sibling, or domestic partner).
- Leave must be taken within 30 days of the date of death.

## 2. Extended Family
- Employees receive **1 paid day** of bereavement leave for the death of an extended family member (grandparent, grandchild, aunt, uncle, or in-law).

## 3. Additional Unpaid Leave
- Additional unpaid bereavement leave may be granted with manager approval, typically up to 5 additional days.
- HR may request documentation (obituary, death certificate) for extended unpaid leave requests.

## 4. Travel Considerations
- Employees traveling internationally for a bereavement may request their leave be extended by up to 2 days to account for travel time, subject to manager approval.

## 5. Requesting Bereavement Leave
- Notify your manager and HR as soon as possible; formal advance notice is not required given the nature of this leave.
- Submit the leave request via the HR portal retroactively if needed.
