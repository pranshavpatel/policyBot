# Bring Your Own Device (BYOD) Policy

This policy covers personal devices used for work purposes; see also Device Policy for company-issued equipment.

## 1. Scope
- Applies to personal smartphones, tablets, and laptops used to access company email, Slack, or other internal systems.
- Does not replace the requirement for a company-issued laptop for your primary work (see Device Policy).

## 2. Enrollment Requirements
- Personal devices must be enrolled in the company's Mobile Device Management (MDM) profile before accessing company email or data.
- A passcode or biometric lock must be enabled on any personal device used for work.

## 3. Data Handling
- Company data must not be stored in personal cloud storage (personal iCloud, personal Google Drive, etc.).
- Screenshots or copies of confidential company data must not be saved to personal photo libraries.

## 4. Loss or Theft
- A lost or stolen personal device that has access to company data must be reported to IT within **24 hours**, same as a company-issued device (see Device Policy).
- IT may remotely wipe the company data partition (not the entire personal device) upon loss report.

## 5. Offboarding
- Company data and the MDM profile will be removed from personal devices upon termination or role change (see Offboarding Policy).

## 6. Reimbursement
- BYOD is voluntary and unpaid; the company does not reimburse the purchase of personal devices. A stipend for company-required software licenses may be available — check with your manager.
