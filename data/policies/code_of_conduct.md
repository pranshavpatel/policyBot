# Code of Conduct

## 1. Core Principles
- Act with integrity, treat colleagues and customers with respect, and comply with all applicable laws.
- Avoid conflicts of interest; disclose any outside business relationships that could conflict with company interests to HR.

## 2. Respectful Workplace
- Harassment, discrimination, and retaliation of any kind are prohibited — see Anti-Harassment Policy for the full reporting process.
- Disagreements should be raised constructively through your manager or HR, not through public criticism of colleagues.

## 3. Confidentiality
- Confidential company and customer information must not be shared outside the company (see Data Security Policy).
- Do not discuss unreleased product plans, financials, or M&A activity outside of approved channels.

## 4. Gifts and Business Courtesies
- Gifts from vendors or partners valued over **$100** must be declined or reported to your manager.

## 5. Reporting Violations
- Violations of this Code of Conduct can be reported to your manager, HR, or the anonymous ethics hotline.
- Retaliation against anyone who reports a good-faith concern is strictly prohibited and is itself a violation of this policy.

## 6. Consequences
- Violations may result in disciplinary action up to and including termination, consistent with the Performance Review Policy's disciplinary process.
