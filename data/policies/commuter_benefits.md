# Commuter Benefits

## 1. Pre-Tax Commuter Account
- Employees may set aside up to the IRS pre-tax limit per month for transit or parking expenses via payroll deduction.

## 2. Transit Subsidy
- The company subsidizes **50%** of public transit costs, up to $75/month, on top of the pre-tax election.

## 3. Bike Program
- A one-time **$150** reimbursement is available for bike purchase or repair for employees who bike commute at least 3 days/week, submitted via the Finance tool.

## 4. Parking
- On-site parking is free for employees who drive; no subsidy is provided for off-site paid parking.

## 5. Eligibility
- Available to all full-time employees at locations with an on-site office; fully remote employees are not eligible for commuter benefits.
