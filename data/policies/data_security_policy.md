# Data Security Policy

Broad data-handling and security rules; see also Password Policy and Device Policy for specific mechanics.

## 1. Data Classification
- Company data is classified as Public, Internal, Confidential, or Restricted.
- Confidential and Restricted data must never be shared outside the company without written approval from your manager and Legal.

## 2. Access Control
- Access to systems and data is granted on a least-privilege basis — only what's needed for your role.
- All access requires a strong password (see Password Policy) and, for sensitive systems, MFA (see MFA Policy).

## 3. Device Requirements
- Confidential or Restricted data may only be accessed from MDM-managed devices (see Device Policy) or MDM-enrolled personal devices (see BYOD Policy).

## 4. Encryption
- All company laptops use full-disk encryption by default, managed via MDM.
- Data in transit to internal systems must go through the VPN (see Remote Access Policy) or an approved encrypted connection.

## 5. Incident Reporting
- Suspected data breaches, phishing attempts, or lost devices with company data must be reported to IT Security within **1 hour** of discovery, via `#it-support` or the security hotline.
- This is a stricter and separate timeline from the general 24-hour lost/stolen device reporting window in Device Policy, which applies to the device itself rather than a suspected breach.

## 6. Third-Party Tools
- New software or SaaS tools that will store or process company data require Security review before adoption (see Software Installation Policy).
