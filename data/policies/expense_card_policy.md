# Corporate Expense Card Policy

See also Reimbursement Policy for out-of-pocket expenses and Travel Policy for travel-specific rules.

## 1. Eligibility
- Employees who travel regularly or manage a team budget may request a corporate expense card through Finance.

## 2. Permitted Use
- Corporate cards may be used for business travel, client meals, software subscriptions approved by your manager, and team events under $500.
- Personal purchases on a corporate card are strictly prohibited, even if reimbursed later.

## 3. Reconciliation
- Corporate card transactions must be reconciled with receipts in the Finance tool within **7 days** of the transaction — shorter than the general 30-day reimbursement window because the company has already paid the charge.

## 4. Spending Limits
- Default monthly limit is $3,000; higher limits require Finance approval.
- Single transactions over $1,000 require pre-approval except for pre-approved travel booked through the travel portal (see Travel Policy).

## 5. Lost or Stolen Cards
- Report a lost or stolen corporate card to Finance immediately; a replacement is typically issued within 5 business days.

## 6. Card Return
- Corporate cards must be returned or destroyed as part of offboarding (see Offboarding Policy).
