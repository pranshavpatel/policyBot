# Health Insurance Details

Full plan detail; see also Benefits Overview for the day-1 eligibility summary.

## 1. Coverage Start
- Health, dental, and vision coverage begins on **Day 1** of employment, with no waiting period.

## 2. Plan Tiers
- Three medical plan tiers are offered: Basic PPO, Standard PPO, and High-Deductible Health Plan (HDHP) with HSA.
- Dental and vision are offered as a single bundled add-on plan.

## 3. Premiums
- The company covers 80% of the employee-only premium and 60% of dependent premiums across all tiers.
- Premiums are deducted pre-tax from each paycheck.

## 4. HSA (HDHP plan only)
- Employees enrolled in the HDHP plan may contribute to a Health Savings Account (HSA); the company contributes **$500/year** to employee HSAs as a match.

## 5. Enrollment Windows
- New hires have 30 days from their start date to enroll.
- Open enrollment occurs annually in November for coverage starting the following January.
- Qualifying life events (marriage, birth, adoption) open a 30-day special enrollment window outside the normal cycle.
