# Offboarding Policy

## 1. Notice
- Employees are asked to provide at least 2 weeks' written notice of resignation, submitted to their manager and HR.

## 2. Final Tasks
- Complete a knowledge-transfer handoff with your manager and team.
- Submit any outstanding expense reports (see Reimbursement Policy) before your last day — they cannot be processed after account deactivation.

## 3. Device and Access Return
- Company laptops and any other company-issued equipment must be returned on or before the last day (see Device Policy).
- Personal devices enrolled under BYOD will have the company MDM profile and data removed (see BYOD Policy).
- All system access, VPN, and MFA enrollments are revoked at end of day on the last day of employment.

## 4. Final Pay and Benefits
- Final paycheck, including unused accrued PTO payout, is issued per state law timelines.
- Health insurance coverage continues through the end of the month of departure; COBRA continuation information is mailed within 14 days.
- Unvested 401(k) matching contributions are forfeited per the vesting schedule (see 401(k) Retirement Policy).

## 5. Tuition Reimbursement Clawback
- If tuition reimbursement was received within the prior 12 months, a prorated repayment may be deducted from final pay (see Tuition Reimbursement Policy).

## 6. Exit Interview
- HR will schedule an optional exit interview before your last day.
