# Onboarding Policy

## 1. Before Day 1
- IT provisions a company laptop (see Device Policy) and VPN/SSO credentials (see Remote Access Policy) before your start date.
- Offer letter, I-9, and benefits enrollment paperwork must be completed via the HR portal before day 1.

## 2. Day 1
- MFA enrollment is required before system access is activated (see MFA Policy).
- New hires complete a security and data-handling orientation covering the Password Policy and Data Security Policy.

## 3. First 30 Days
- Health, dental, and vision benefits are already active (coverage begins Day 1, see Health Insurance Details) — no action needed unless changing plan tier, which must be done within the 30-day enrollment window.
- 401(k) enrollment is available starting day 1 (see 401(k) Retirement Policy).
- New hires complete their first 30 days on-site or hybrid before being eligible for full remote work (see Remote Work Policy).

## 4. Manager Responsibilities
- Assign an onboarding buddy for the new hire's first 2 weeks.
- Schedule a 30/60/90-day check-in per the Performance Review Policy cadence.

## 5. Required Reading
- New hires must acknowledge having read the Code of Conduct and Anti-Harassment Policy within the first week.
