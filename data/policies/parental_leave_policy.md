# Parental Leave Policy

This policy provides full detail on parental leave; see also Leave Policy §8 for a summary.

## 1. Eligibility
- Available to all full-time employees regardless of tenure for the birth, adoption, or foster placement of a child.
- Both the primary and secondary caregiver are eligible, regardless of gender.

## 2. Duration
- **Primary caregiver**: up to **12 weeks** of paid parental leave.
- **Secondary caregiver**: up to **4 weeks** of paid parental leave.
- Leave may be taken continuously or split into two blocks within the first 12 months after the birth/placement date.

## 3. Requesting Parental Leave
- Must be requested at least **30 days in advance** when the birth/placement date is foreseeable.
- Submit the request via the HR portal, noting expected start date and caregiver designation (primary or secondary).
- Manager and HR approval are both required.

## 4. Pay and Benefits During Leave
- Parental leave is paid at 100% of base salary.
- Health, dental, and vision benefits continue uninterrupted during parental leave.
- Parental leave does not draw down standard PTO balances — it is a separate leave type.

## 5. Returning to Work
- Employees are guaranteed return to the same or an equivalent role and level.
- A phased return (reduced schedule for up to 2 weeks) may be arranged with manager approval.

## 6. Interaction with Other Leave
- Parental leave runs concurrently with any state or federal family leave entitlement (see Leave Policy §13, Compliance).
- Unused standard PTO (see Leave Policy §2) may be used to extend time away after parental leave ends.
