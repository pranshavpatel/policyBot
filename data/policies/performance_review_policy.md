# Performance Review Policy

## 1. Review Cadence
- Formal performance reviews occur twice a year: a mid-year check-in and a year-end review tied to compensation decisions.
- New hires receive an additional 30/60/90-day check-in cycle (see Onboarding Policy).

## 2. Rating Scale
- Employees are rated on a 5-point scale: Exceeds Expectations, Strong Performance, Meets Expectations, Needs Improvement, Unsatisfactory.

## 3. Goal Setting
- Goals are set at the start of each review cycle, aligned with team and company objectives, using the HR portal's goal-tracking tool.

## 4. Compensation Review
- Year-end reviews inform annual merit increases and bonus eligibility; there is no guaranteed annual increase.

## 5. Performance Improvement Plans (PIPs)
- A Needs Improvement or Unsatisfactory rating may result in a formal Performance Improvement Plan, typically **60 days**, with defined milestones.
- Failure to meet PIP milestones may result in disciplinary action up to and including termination (see Code of Conduct §6).

## 6. Peer Feedback
- 360-degree peer feedback is collected as an input to the year-end review but is not shared verbatim with the employee being reviewed.
