# Remote Access Policy

Covers VPN and remote-access setup and rules; see also VPN Reset for the password-reset procedure specifically.

## 1. Who Needs VPN Access
- Any employee accessing internal systems, file shares, or internal tools from outside the office network requires an active VPN connection.
- VPN access is provisioned automatically for full-time employees and contractors on their first day (see Onboarding Policy).

## 2. Setting Up VPN
- VPN client credentials are issued via the SSO portal on day one.
- Multi-factor authentication (MFA) is required to establish a VPN session, in addition to your SSO password.

## 3. Session Rules
- VPN sessions automatically time out after **8 hours** of inactivity and must be re-established.
- Only one active VPN session per employee is permitted at a time.
- Split-tunneling (routing only some traffic through the VPN) is disabled by default; contact IT if your role requires an exception.

## 4. Approved Locations
- VPN access from within the country of employment is unrestricted.
- Sustained VPN access from a different country for more than 2 consecutive weeks requires the same approval as the Remote Work Policy's international remote work rule.

## 5. Troubleshooting
- For a forgotten VPN password, follow the steps in VPN Reset.
- For persistent connection failures unrelated to your password, contact IT via `#it-support` with your device ID.
