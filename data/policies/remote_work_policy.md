# Remote Work Policy

## 1. Eligibility
- Employees whose role does not require regular on-site presence may work remotely with manager approval.
- New hires must complete their first 30 days on-site or in a hybrid arrangement before transitioning to full remote work.

## 2. Equipment and Security
- Remote employees must use company-issued, MDM-managed laptops (see Device Policy).
- VPN must be connected for any access to internal systems or company data while working remotely (see Device Policy, VPN Reset).
- Home Wi-Fi networks must use WPA2 or WPA3 encryption at minimum.

## 3. Availability
- Remote employees must be reachable during their team's designated core hours (typically 10am-3pm local time).
- Time zone differences must be accounted for when scheduling meetings and requesting leave (see Leave Policy §12).

## 4. Home Office Stipend
- Remote employees receive a one-time **$500** home office setup stipend, submitted as a reimbursement (see Reimbursement Policy) within 60 days of their remote start date.

## 5. Performance and Communication
- Remote employees are held to the same performance standards as on-site employees (see Performance Review Policy).
- Cameras should be on for team meetings unless otherwise agreed with your manager.

## 6. International Remote Work
- Working remotely from outside your home country for more than 2 consecutive weeks requires prior approval from HR and Legal, due to tax and employment law implications.
