# 401(k) Retirement Policy

Full plan detail; see also Benefits Overview for the match summary.

## 1. Eligibility
- All full-time employees are eligible to enroll starting their first day of employment.
- Part-time employees become eligible after working 1,000 hours in a 12-month period.

## 2. Company Match
- The company matches **up to 4%** of employee contributions, dollar for dollar.
- The match is calculated per pay period, not just at year-end.

## 3. Vesting Schedule
- Employee contributions are always 100% vested immediately.
- Company matching contributions vest over 3 years: 0% at hire, 50% at 1 year, 100% at 2 years of service.

## 4. Contribution Limits
- Employees may contribute up to the IRS annual limit via pre-tax or Roth 401(k) contributions.
- Automatic contribution increases (1% per year) can be opted into during enrollment.

## 5. Changing Contributions
- Contribution percentages can be changed at any time through the retirement plan portal, effective the next pay period.
