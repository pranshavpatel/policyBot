# Business Travel Policy

Covers booking and expensing business travel; see also Reimbursement Policy for the general expense process and Expense Card Policy for corporate card use.

## 1. Booking
- Flights and hotels should be booked through the company's approved travel portal whenever possible.
- Economy class is standard for flights under 6 hours; premium economy is permitted for flights over 6 hours with manager approval.

## 2. Per Diem
- Meals while traveling are covered up to **$75/day** domestic, **$100/day** international, in lieu of itemized meal receipts under those amounts.

## 3. Ground Transportation
- Rideshare, taxi, and rental car expenses are reimbursable with receipts.
- Rental cars should be a standard or compact class unless traveling with 3+ colleagues.

## 4. Submission Window
- Travel expenses must be submitted within **14 days** of trip completion — shorter than the standard 30-day window in Reimbursement Policy, because travel receipts are more likely to be lost the longer they wait.
- Submit via the Finance tool under the "Travel" category, itemized by day.

## 5. Approval
- Trips over $2,000 in estimated cost require pre-trip approval from your manager and Finance.
- Reimbursement itself still requires manager approval per the standard process in Reimbursement Policy.
