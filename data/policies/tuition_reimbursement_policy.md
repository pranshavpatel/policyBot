# Tuition Reimbursement Policy

Covers reimbursement for job-related coursework and degree programs; see also Benefits Overview for the annual learning budget figure, and Reimbursement Policy for the general expense process.

## 1. Eligible Programs
- Courses, certifications, and degree programs must be job-related or contribute to a reasonable career path within the company.
- Pre-approval from your manager is required before enrolling.

## 2. Annual Cap
- Up to **$1,000 per year** is available for tuition reimbursement, drawn from the same annual learning budget described in Benefits Overview.

## 3. Grade Requirement
- A grade of B or higher (or "Pass" for pass/fail courses) is required for reimbursement of degree-program coursework.
- Certifications and workshops without letter grades are exempt from this requirement.

## 4. Submission Process
- Unlike general expense reimbursement (30 days, see Reimbursement Policy), tuition reimbursement must be submitted within **45 days** of course completion, along with a grade report or certificate of completion.
- Submit via the Finance tool under the "Tuition" category.

## 5. Repayment Clause
- If you voluntarily leave the company within 12 months of receiving tuition reimbursement, you may be required to repay a prorated portion.
