# Wellness Program

See also Benefits Overview for the monthly stipend figure.

## 1. Wellness Stipend
- Employees receive a **$50/month** wellness stipend, usable for gym memberships, fitness classes, or wellness apps.
- Submitted as a recurring reimbursement via the Finance tool; receipts required.

## 2. Mental Health Resources
- All employees and their dependents have access to an Employee Assistance Program (EAP) offering up to 6 free counseling sessions per year.
- The EAP is confidential and not visible to managers or HR.

## 3. On-Site Programs
- Weekly on-site yoga and meditation sessions are offered at company headquarters.
- Standing desks are available upon request through Facilities at no cost.

## 4. Annual Wellness Challenge
- An optional annual step-count challenge runs each spring, with small prizes for participation.

## 5. Preventive Care
- Annual physicals and preventive screenings are covered at 100% under all health insurance plan tiers (see Health Insurance Details), with no copay.
